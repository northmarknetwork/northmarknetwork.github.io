Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

This policy explains how Northmark Uploader (the "App"), an internal tool operated by Northmark Network ("we", "us"), handles information. The App is used only by the Northmark Network team to publish and manage videos on our own YouTube channel(s), such as Northmark Empires. It is not offered to the public, and no other person can sign in to it.

YouTube API Services

The App uses YouTube API Services. By using the App you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. YouTube and Google handle data according to the Google Privacy Policy.

What the App accesses

After the channel owner signs in with Google and grants permission, the App can:

  • upload videos, thumbnails and caption files to the authorised channel;
  • read and update the title, description, tags, privacy status and publishing time of videos on that channel;
  • read basic channel information (channel name and handle) to confirm which channel is connected.

The App does not access viewers' personal data, comments, subscribers' data, private messages or analytics of other people.

How information is stored

  • The Google OAuth refresh token is stored only on the operator's own computer, outside any shared location. It is never sent to any server other than Google's.
  • The App has no server, no database and no user accounts of its own. It sends requests directly from the operator's computer to Google/YouTube.
  • Video files and metadata are prepared and kept locally by Northmark Network.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share any data obtained through YouTube API Services with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising. No analytics or tracking tools are used by the App.

Retention and deletion

Data obtained through the API is used only to carry out the requested action and is not stored beyond what is needed for that action. The stored refresh token is deleted when access is revoked or the App is no longer used. You can revoke the App's access at any time in your Google Account under Security → Third-party apps with account access.
